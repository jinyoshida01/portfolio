About.

22.12.2024 19:06  34°58′2″N 135°46′22″E

Hi, I’m Jin, a Multidisciplinary Designer based in Melbourne holding a Bachelor of Communication Design (Honours), crafting purposeful, visually engaging work across print, digital, 3D, and web.

I’m passionate about crafting meaningful design solutions that are both visually engaging and purpose-driven, no matter the medium.

My experience covers everything from traditional print and digital graphics, to more specialised areas like 3D rendering and web projects.

I take pride in my adaptability and confidence in approaching any task with a problem-solving mindset.
