Overview

Meal kits you actually make yourself.

Woofpack is a brand concept centered on providing dog owners with fresh recipes and ingredients to create meals at home. It offers a hands-on, engaging experience with full control over the preparation process.

Drawing on both my illustration and design skills, I drafted an app UI, website landing page, recipe brochures, packaging, and various marketing materials to create a cohesive and unified brand experience.

Colour & illustration

Earthy, but not earnest.

The visual identity features earthy green tones paired with brush-style doodle illustrations, conveying a natural, healthy aesthetic with a playful, handcrafted feel.
